Research

My research connects organismal biology, microbial ecology, biogeochemistry, and computation to understand how biological systems respond to environmental change.

01 — Plant–Microbe Interactions

I investigate how plant identity, environmental history, soil conditions, and microbial communities influence plant performance and stress responses. My work includes rhizosphere, rhizoplane, and nodule-associated microbiomes.

02 — Soil Microbial Ecology & Biogeochemistry

I study how microbial communities respond to carbon availability, grazing, nutrient management, and other environmental drivers, with an emphasis on links among microbial activity, carbon cycling, and nutrient transformations.

03 — Multi-omics & Bioinformatics

I integrate metabarcoding, metatranscriptomics, comparative genomics, and other omics approaches with reproducible computational workflows. A major goal is to develop new analytical pipelines that make complex biological datasets easier to interpret.
