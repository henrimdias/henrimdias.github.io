Current research

Projects

Current research projects connecting plant biology, microbial ecology, soil biogeochemistry, environmental change, and multi-omics.

01 — Investigating Molecular Signatures, Organic Matter, and Nutrient Cycling in Responsive Agroecosystems

Investigating how management, plant–microbe–soil interactions, and environmental conditions influence microbial signatures, organic matter dynamics, and carbon and nitrogen cycling across agroecosystems.

Collaboration

Christopher Graham (PI) · Hector Menendez

16S ITS Metatranscriptomics Grazing Stable isotopes Carbon Nitrogen Plant–microbe–soil interactions

02 — SDSU Bison Conservation Innovation Grant (CIG)

Investigating how plant communities, soil properties, and multi-kingdom microbial communities interact within bison-managed grassland ecosystems.

Collaboration

Jeff Martin (PI) · Ashlynn Smith · Rachel Short · Abby Guthmann · Christopher Graham

16S ITS 18S Plant ecology Bison ecology Soil Multi-kingdom microbiome

03 — Drought-Induced Microbial Shifts and Their Influence on Seed Quality and Oil Content in Camelina

Examining how drought alters plant-associated microbial communities and whether these shifts are associated with plant physiology, seed quality, and oil production in camelina.

Collaboration

Christopher Graham (PI) · Paul Umezinwa

16S ITS Plant physiology Drought Soil Camelina

04 — Predicting Microbial Resuscitation and Carbon Allocation from Drought Legacy in Rewetted Grassland Soils

Investigating whether long-term drought history changes microbial preparedness, resuscitation, and the allocation of carbon between microbial growth and respiration following soil rewetting.

Collaboration

Christopher Graham (PI) · Jacqueline Ott · Amy Symstad · Troy Ocheltree

16S ITS Soil biochemistry Carbon Nitrogen Metabolomics Metatranscriptomics Stable isotopes Carbon use efficiency Drought legacy

05 — Northern Great Plains Native Rhizobium Collection

Building and characterizing a culture collection of native rhizobia to investigate genomic diversity, nodulation potential, and plant–microbe associations across the Northern Great Plains.

Collaboration

Christopher Graham (PI) · Taylor Guiot

16S Comparative genomics Oxford Nanopore Bacterial genomics Nodulation Rhizobium
